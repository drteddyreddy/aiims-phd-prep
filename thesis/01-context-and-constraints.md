## 4. AIIMS Guwahati PhD rules (verbatim-checked against the R2 prospectus and the Jul 2026 advertisement; full passages in `thesis/local/04-aiims-rules.md`)

- **Doctoral Committee**: guide submits a panel to the Executive Director within 6 months of registration. Composition: guide, at least one external expert, up to three internal experts, co-guide(s), HoD; a biostatistician is "preferable".
- **Protocol**: submitted to the DC **within 3 months of DC formation and no later than 9 months from registration**; biostatistician advice "should invariably be obtained"; DC recommends; candidate revises; **Dean approves through the guide; then IEC**, with ethics approval "preferably within 3 months" of submission. ## 6. Indian regulatory frame

- **ICMR National Ethical Guidelines 2017**: consent only after CIWA-Ar is low and cognition clear; re-confirm consent; alcohol-dependent inpatients are a vulnerable group.
- **New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules 2019**: an "academic clinical trial" (approved product, new indication, investigator-initiated, non-commercial) needs IEC approval, not CDSCO permission. rTMS or tDCS off-label for AUD is an academic trial of a marketed device. State this reasoning in the protocol.
- **CTRI**: mandatory for regulatory trials, recommended for all others, required by ICMJE journals before first enrolment. Register both lanes prospectively. The CTRI site timed out from your laptop; register from the institute network.
- **SAE reporting**: ICMR 2017 aligns academic trial SAE reporting with NDCT 2019 timelines. Reuse the CBD trial SOP.
- **DPDP Act 2023**: any app or WhatsApp component makes you a data fiduciary. Keep clinical data on institute systems; use messaging only to push content.

## 7. Interaction with the CBD trial

Three possible relationships:

1. **Nested aftercare study.** Participants complete the trial's inpatient phase; after its primary-endpoint window they are offered your intervention under separate consent. Needs steering-committee approval; CBD arm becomes a stratification variable.
2. **Parallel cohort.** Detox inpatients screened out of or declining the trial, or admissions after the trial closes recruitment.
3. **Co-enrolment during the trial's active window.** Avoid unless the PI agrees in writing.

If your seat is Category A PSY02, option 1 is the natural reading of "thesis derived from the project" and is also the one most likely to satisfy the Dean's office. Ask the PI which of 1 or 2 is acceptable (question 4).
