## The bottom line, in eight lines

1. **Both lanes are viable.** You have a continuous inpatient detox cohort, a TMS machine, a guide whose ANRF project is about technology for continuity of care in substance use, and an institution whose own PhD advertisement lists RCTs as thesis projects.
2. **Lane 2 is the stronger thesis.** No trial of the Allen Carr logic exists in alcohol, in a dependent population, or in India, and neither smoking RCT measured the beliefs the method claims to change. Four firsts, five papers, low equipment risk, and it sits inside your guide's programme.
3. **Lane 1 now has an incumbent to beat.** Joshi et al. 2025 (AIIMS, n = 149) cut 3-month relapse from 63.5% to 44% with twice-daily tDCS over five days, a schedule that fits a detox stay and needs no sham coil. An iTBS trial must justify TMS over that. **It also now has a direct precedent:** Hou et al. 2026 already tested accelerated left-DLPFC iTBS against sham in detoxified inpatients (about 20 per arm; craving result not significant, abstinence lasted longer). The 147-person Wake Forest trial (Kinney) has published only its protocol. So Lane 1's novelty is now timing inside the detox admission, adequate power and a hard relapse outcome, and its sample size rises to N ≈ 148 (d = 0.45).
4. **The evidence for both lanes is thinner than the abstracts suggest.** Belgers 2022 has no 12-month effect; Hoven 2023 is null on everything; the iTBS meta-analysis has no alcohol craving trial; neither alcohol iTBS RCT that measured craving (Hou 2026, Durazzo 2026) found a craving effect; Keogan 2019 is positive only under ITT; Frings 2020 has OR 1.38 with a CI that includes 1. The pack now says so.
5. **Recommended structure:** Lane 2 as the thesis, Lane 1 as a small sham-controlled pilot in years 2–3 (or a double-blind tDCS replication-extension if the TMS route closes).
6. **The seat may decide the nesting question.** The July 2026 advertisement lists PSY02 as a Category A seat inside the CBD trial. Your guide says the thesis can be independent; get that in writing from the Dean's office.
7. **The clock is specific.** Doctoral Committee by month 6, protocol to DC by month 9 from registration, DC reports every 6 months, and two papers with IF > 1.5 (or one > 3) before submission.
8. **The Guwahati IEC wants validated questionnaires in English, Hindi and Assamese**, so scale translation is a year-1 task for either lane.
